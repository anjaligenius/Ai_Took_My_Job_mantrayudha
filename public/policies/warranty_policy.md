# NovaMart Warranty Policy

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Policy | Warranty Policy |
| Version | 1.0 (single version) |
| **Effective date** | **2026-01-01** |
| Applies to | All products sold by NovaMart |

## 1. Coverage
- Every product carries a manufacturer warranty of `products.warranty_months` months (6-36 months depending on the product).
- The warranty **starts on the delivery date** (`orders.actual_delivery_date`) and ends on the same calendar day `warranty_months` later.
- It covers **manufacturing defects** that appear under normal use.

## 2. Not covered
Physical damage (drops, cracked screens caused by the user), liquid damage, burn marks, unauthorised repair or opening, software modifications, damage from non-NovaMart accessories, consumables (ear tips, straps, batteries after 6 months of heavy cycles unless defective from the start) and cosmetic wear.

## 3. Warranty vs return/refund/replacement
1. Inside the return/defect window of the applicable Refund Policy, the customer is entitled to **return/replacement/refund** (see those policies). Use those first.
2. After the defect window but inside the warranty period, the remedy is **warranty service**: repair, or replacement if the service centre declares the unit irreparable. A refund is **not** offered.
3. After the warranty ends, the customer is offered **paid repair** via an authorised service centre. No free repair, replacement or refund.

## 4. How a claim works
1. Confirm order, product and issue (and serial number where available).
2. Check dates: `delivery date + warranty_months`.
3. Register a warranty claim (ticket category `warranty`); schedule a service pickup or ask the customer to visit a service centre.
4. Service centre diagnoses within 3 business days; repair completes within 15 business days. If the same fault repeats after two repairs, or the repair exceeds 30 days, a replacement unit is arranged.
5. A replacement unit carries the remaining warranty or 3 months, whichever is longer.

## 5. What agents may and may not promise
- May: register the claim, explain coverage, share the end date, offer paid-repair options.
- May not: promise a replacement or refund before the service centre report; approve warranty for physical/liquid damage; extend warranty beyond the end date.

## 6. Safety
Swollen batteries, overheating during charging, burning smell or smoke are **safety incidents**: instruct the customer to stop using and charging the device immediately and escalate as critical (Customer Escalation Policy). They override normal windows.
